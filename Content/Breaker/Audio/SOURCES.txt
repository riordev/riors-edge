Placeholder combat sounds. All four are single events cut (slice, fade,
downmix, normalize; kill additionally pitched down a third by sample-rate
declaration) from CC0 recordings on OpenGameArt. Cutting was scripted, so
the edits are reproducible.

weapon_fire.wav  from "Black Powder.wav", first shot
hit_confirm.wav  from "22 Pistol.wav", first transient (90 ms)
kill_confirm.wav from "22 Magnum.wav", first shot, pitched down
  all three: "Gunshots" by kurt, CC0,
  https://opengameart.org/content/gunshots

take_hit.wav     from "death pain grunts.wav", first grunt
  "grunts of male death and pain" by thebardofblasphemy, CC0,
  https://opengameart.org/content/grunts-male-death-and-pain

ability_cast.wav is kenney/sci-fi-sounds/forceField_003.wav verbatim
(Kenney Sci-Fi Sounds, CC0, converted set — see kenney/SOURCES.txt).
Chosen headless by measurement, not by ear: 0.956 s, mono, and the
spectral centroid RISES 593 -> 1233 Hz across the clip, which keeps the
synth default's one design rule — the ability cue is the set's only
rising sweep while all four combat verbs sweep down. The owner A/Bs the
actual sound.
