## Primitive contracts

- Resource efficiency applies once before class price/window rewrites, to every class; affordability, HUD and actual debit read the same live cost. Caster retains its negative resource floor and Unmake window.
- Generic generation scales normal earned loop income. Direct grants and refunds remain unchanged; existing generation suspension remains authoritative. Scrap snapshots earned credit at enqueue and scales throughput, while destruction refunds retain their original amount. Flat regeneration and no-decay are distinct rules.
- Sprint speed has its own lane, leaving walking unchanged. Acceleration scales the engine acceleration limit. Jump-height bonuses scale impulse by their square root, preserving the authored base and existing gravity curve. Additional air jumps do not acquire Swift's innate directional rewrite.
- Ledge speed snapshots traversal duration at entry. Safe-fall bonuses are authored in metres and extend the distance threshold, leaving the damage-per-excess-metre rule unchanged. Respec removes subsequent benefits without retiming an active traversal.
- Conduction adds an independent 40-percentage-point cost contribution per successful committed cast; each fades linearly over five seconds. Apply the sum after existing class price/window rewrites. Failed attempts and cancellation presses do not count; free successful casts do. Existing cooldowns age normally while bypassed. Death or losing the rule clears contributions. Quotes and payment share a commit snapshot; refunds use the actual amount paid.
- Weapon range and magazine bonuses join the matching gear Increased bucket. Buying capacity grants headroom, not ammunition; respec returns excess loaded rounds to reserve. Reserve bonuses scale starting supply, pickup fractions and the existing reserve cap. Reload/swap clocks and rocket speed/area snapshot when started/fired. Splash area scales radius by its square root; pierce-loss reduction reduces the lost fraction (70% retention becomes74.5% at15% less loss).
- Explicit role/lane/sector metadata drives the replacement layout. Invalid metadata fails visibly instead of guessing old tiers. The187-node structural layout fixture is independent of live roster activation and runtime effect completeness.
