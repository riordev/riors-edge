- **Combat:** cheaper, faster Caster Cleave with measured direct damage close to starter rifle throughput at short range; finite Rot funding and native clock checks; Provoke and Standing Order behavior; Warden front shield stays broken through revival. Sprint/shoot exclusion and delayed base regeneration were already present before this pass.

No measured silent nodes does not establish that every build feels useful.

All seven destination cards now fit at720p with shorter setting/objective descriptions. Static captures do not replace interactive focus testing.

Red Basin has recorder recovery/extraction; Station Zero has a persistent Containment Custodian hunt. Port Meridian adds airport terminal, apron and hangar pockets at levels34/36/38 with three guarded supply recoveries. Broken Coast adds a flat shore, stranded boat, broken pier and beacon at levels42/44/46 with guarded supply recovery. Shatterpoint adds ruined street, civic square and overpass market districts at levels48/50/52 with different ranged/Warden/Skirmisher compositions. All remain blockout environments.
- **Scenery:** Anchor gatehouses, terraces and work areas; Fernhall pipeworks and foliage perimeter. Supported-material rendering restored for imported foliage. Weapons, arms, enemy weakpoints and overall art cohesion still need substantial work.
- **Cooperative sandbox:** opt-in isolated host/guest combat, owner slot metadata, real Slipcut local/server payment and prediction cleanup, real remote weapon damage and replicated enemy death; server-owned guest death/respawn with client input restoration. Disposable profiles do not write ordinary character/account saves. Full campaign multiplayer is not implemented.
