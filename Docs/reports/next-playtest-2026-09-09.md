# Next large playtest — change summary

This is a running handoff, not a claim that the seven-step programme is complete. Use the current DESK and STATE for the exact latest build and unresolved measurements.

## Main changes to try

- **Combat:** abilities wind up — 24 of 34 now take a moment to land, Cleave 0.35s among them, its Mana is spent on the keypress and never returned, and damage interrupts the cast; movement and defensive verbs stay instant. Base walk speed raised 595 to 640 against an owner "clunky" report, with sprint deliberately unchanged; a landing now latches the boosted speed ceiling into its bleed, so a sprint/jump/slide/jump chain converges instead of ratcheting; Cleave reach raised 450 to 650 cm, though the sweep still reads centre to centre and so lands short of what it looks like by both capsule radii; cheaper, faster Caster Cleave with measured direct damage close to starter rifle throughput at short range; finite Rot funding and native clock checks; Provoke and Standing Order behavior; Warden front shield stays broken through revival; Bloodline now ends on death and cannot resume its former healing after revival; Ground Zero likewise cancels its armed plunge on death. Sprint/shoot exclusion and delayed base regeneration were already present before this pass.
- **Progression:** complete 22-wedge Core roster remains active; paid Hold/Sightline/Slipcut/Overdrive/Cadence Break/Hard Stop Afterimage and Survivor Core-point delivery checks; Swift starts with Slipcut and an intentionally vacant second slot, with one-time retired-Skim refunds. Hard Stop protection gains its half-strength tail while Spend to Live immunity ends normally; removal/respec/death cannot restore old protection. No measured silent nodes does not establish that every build feels useful.
- **Loot:** item levels 1 to 10 now carry no equip requirement, so the opening hour's drops can actually be worn; saved special items keep their existing affixes through migration; guarded supply lockers produce a real ordinary loot pickup after nearby defenders die. Category balance and all legal special-perk combinations remain broader acceptance work.
- **Interface:** M opens the local map anywhere and the map is fast travel — one way home from any instance, the full list from the Anchor, refused in combat. Levelling now has a cue — a rising two-tone sting and a gold ground ring at the feet, beside the banner that already existed and was not felt. The Core overview names itself again — every text block on that canvas was gated off for the shipped role layout, so the wheel drew 187 anonymous markers; wedge names, sector names, the hub mark and visible sector divisions are back, sized to the fit zoom. Core overview links remain visible and purchasable gateways now stand out from locked nodes; focused Precision and all three 12-node Caster doctrine boards checked at 720p. Ability rows and inventory destruction prose wrap within their actual available width; single-item discard reports actual success/failure; vacant slot filler removed; clear nearby recorder/cache actions; distant services no longer display unavailable action keys; boss and Volatile warnings respect cover and screen edges; damage-label placement now also avoids champion rank diamonds, and the player DAMAGE callout no longer covers enemy Rot timers. The dev sandbox distinguishes gym-only difficulty controls from fixed regions and reports the actual forced class. All seven destination cards now fit at 720p with shorter setting/objective descriptions. Escape returns from Character Sheet and Local Map to the root menu. Static captures do not replace interactive focus testing.
- **Destinations:** Red Basin, Station Zero, Port Meridian, Broken Coast and Shatterpoint are real fixed-level prototype regions, each with three districts, enemies, supply lockers, maps and return gates. Red Basin has recorder recovery/extraction; Station Zero has a persistent Containment Custodian hunt. Port Meridian adds airport terminal, apron and hangar pockets at levels 34/36/38 with optional guarded supply recoveries and a physical ground-crew escort across all three districts. Broken Coast adds a flat shore, stranded boat, broken pier and beacon at levels 42/44/46 with optional guarded supply recovery and a coastal uplink objective. Start the console at Signal Point and remain within 7m and in sight for 12s; leaving or dying resets it, completion persists and directs return. Shatterpoint adds ruined street, civic square and overpass market districts at levels 48/50/52 with different ranged/Warden/Skirmisher compositions. Shatterpoint's three required cache objectives are now visible and trackable immediately on arrival. The HUD now names the nearest authored district and fixed level as you move through each region. All remain blockout environments.
- **Scenery:** Anchor gatehouses, terraces and work areas; Fernhall pipeworks and foliage perimeter. Supported-material rendering restored for imported foliage. Weapons, arms, enemy weakpoints and overall art cohesion still need substantial work.
- **Cooperative sandbox:** opt-in isolated host/guest combat, owner slot metadata, real Slipcut local/server payment and prediction cleanup, real remote weapon damage and replicated enemy death; server-owned guest death/respawn with client input restoration. Disposable profiles do not write ordinary character/account saves. Full campaign multiplayer is not implemented.

## Suggested route

1. Start in Anchor and check service readability, equipment/ability assignment and menu focus.
2. Compare starter Caster Cleave, Rot and an earned Fracture against ordinary packs and a tougher target; report range, resource downtime and damage-number readability separately.
3. Play Fernhall's pockets, Watchkeeper and guarded lockers. Check the existing campaign Rift through reward and return without developer shortcuts. Native acceptance now waits through its real wave breathers; a complete player-driven door-to-return loop remains to be played.
4. Travel to Red Basin, recover the recorder, extract it and return; revisit after a normal save/load. Then hunt Station Zero's Custodian, optionally clear lockers and return. At Port Meridian, meet the ground crew, guide them through the three defended checkpoints and reach Maintenance Hangar; try separation and death/retry. At Broken Coast, restore the Signal Point uplink and test leaving its working radius, retrying and returning after completion.
5. Spend and refund Core/Doctrine points across contrasting builds. Report a specific node and trigger when something seems inactive.
6. If testing coop, use the documented isolated sandbox. Campaign progression, shared loot, rejoin and broad class support remain outside its verified scope.

## Known limitations

- The seven-step pass remains open: complete kits, legal build balance, generated return Rifts, natural campaign leveling, interactive UI acceptance and polished audio/art are not complete.
- Warden arrival fix is parked under the five-minute bug limit. Its initial native fixture dies to a level-one slam before proving spacing; no candidate production change was shipped.
- Blackout Afterimage needs source-owned slow/damage contributions before extending its tail safely.
- Overhaul Bench Work removal cleanup is parked: callback interruption during conversion settlement can leave the old window/listeners active. The candidate was not shipped; exact capacity refunds alone did not prove full cleanup.
- Caster coop expansion is parked after two real Cleave activation rejections; metadata/native isolation passed, actual combat did not. The first failed run logged player death 25ms before the cast request. Existing Swift network verification passed an unchanged rerun; these receipts still do not prove encounter balance.
- The new enclosed Substation candidate is parked after its arrival-floor runtime check failed. Current campaign Rifts still use the existing environment.
- Audio has installed main samples and authored reaction synth fallbacks. Native sample/queue checks do not establish actual speaker/headphone output.
- Existing expected-red balance findings remain visible; pins were not moved to manufacture a green result.
