## The enhanced-dash node's shape (ORDERS ruling 1, reported before authoring)

- **Swift node, not Core.** It is Swift's second free verb; a Core node would
  hand it to all five classes.
- **Stat target `DashCooldown`**, IncreasedPercent, into the wired
  `DashCooldownReduction` lane. In the vocabulary that exists, "enhanced dash"
  means a shorter dash cooldown: charges, distance and i-frames are not
  authorable — each needs Movement/KIT plumbing that does not exist, and
  authoring against it is the rule Design discipline forbids.
- **"Granted at level one" has no mechanism yet.** Nothing auto-grants a node:
  every rank is purchased. The cheap shape is a rank seeded free at class
  choice — `ChoosePermanentClassById` writes the node at rank 1, cost 0 — and
  it needs two properties ruled with it: a respec must not refund what was
  never paid for, and the board must draw it owned-and-unrefundable.
- **Question:** confirm the seeded-free-rank grant and the cooldown reading of
  "enhanced", and the magnitude gets authored (O2) in the next cycle.

## Does the rider path carry a More bucket? (the Collapse question, O125's bullet)

Reported in full last cycle. The short form: hit-time Mores cannot enter the
strongest-three sort (membership is unrepresentable in the request's two-float
split, and the sheet's "N / 3 MORE" stops meaning anything), but they already
exist under the window law — the outgoing chain spends ceiling headroom, not a
slot. A rider More under that law is one clamp at the recomposition site.
**Question:** is partial payment acceptable — full ×1.30 on an ability build
(composing 2.1632 of 2.197), ×1.1355 on a weapon build at 1.9349, nothing at
saturation, tooltip-stated? If yes, Collapse's intended shape is lawful under
O34 as already worded. If no, target-gated lines are Increased-only permanently
and Collapse gets a different design.
