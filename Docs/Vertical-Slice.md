# Vertical slice

The first slice proves movement and item feedback in one graybox arena: sprint, dash, slide, mantle, and short wall movement; three weapons; three enemies, one elite, one boss; basic GAS health/damage/cooldowns; randomized loot with three legendaries; about 15 skill nodes; and save/load. Grapple is excluded.

Current combat-sandbox progress: the rifle, scattergun, and marksman archetypes are playable through a two-weapon loadout; three simple patrol/chase/attack enemies exercise incoming damage and player recovery; and the runtime gym covers core movement distances. Elite modifiers, boss behavior, loot, progression, save/resume, and authored presentation remain later slice work.

Done means a fresh player can enter, learn movement, defeat enemies, equip randomized loot, choose a skill, defeat the boss, quit, and resume—with a clean clone building on both macOS and Windows.
