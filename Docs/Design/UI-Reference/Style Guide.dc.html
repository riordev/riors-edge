RIOR'S EDGE / UI SPECIFICATION / V1.0

FIELDPLATE

The Breaker interface is stamped sheet metal, not glass. Every panel reads as a plate bolted to a chassis that has been carried through wet ruin — square shoulders, hard edges, a single accent rail marking function the way paint marks equipment in a motor pool. Rift technology never softens that: it is quarantined into thin instrument lines and object-marking teal, so the ominous part of the screen is always the part that belongs to the anomaly, never the housing around it.

Shape & space

8px base grid. Nothing lands off it.
ACCENT RAIL — the signature
Kinetic surge
Left rail: identity. Which system owns this panel.
Purchase confirmed
Top rail: transient status. Reserved for events and alerts.
Rail is 3px, full-bleed to the panel's edge, no inset, no radius on the rail itself. One rail per panel — a panel with two rails has no meaning. Nested panels do not repeat the parent's rail colour.
RADII & BORDERS
0px
2px
4px
0px for structural zones and grid cells. 2px default — panels, cards, buttons, slots. 4px only on ability squares and squircle icon tiles, where the eye expects a bevelled plate. Never above 4px.

Borders: 1px #1F3047 at rest, 1px #2A3E58 for emphasis, 2px accent for selected, 3px for the rail. There is no 2px neutral border.

Scale is 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64. 12 and 32 are not tokens. Minimum hit target 44px; HUD ability squares 64px.
04

Motion

Click a card to fire it.
PANEL TRANSITION
180ms · cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)
Panels slide 16px along their rail axis and fade 0→1. Out is 120ms linear, no travel — equipment closes faster than it opens. No scale, ever: plates do not grow.
PLATE ONLINE
PURCHASE CONFIRM
90ms in · 260ms out · ease-out
Border snaps to gold on frame one — no ease in, the commit must feel mechanical. Rank pip fills, then gold decays back to the owned colour over 260ms. Total 350ms, never queued: spam-buying reads as a stutter of snaps.
RANK 3 / 5
DAMAGE FEEDBACK
40ms pop · 520ms rise · ease-out
Numbers spawn at 115% for 40ms, settle to 100%, rise 40px and fade over the last 200ms. Crits spawn at 140% and hold 60ms. Health-loss bars use 0ms drain and 400ms ease-out chip recovery — harm is instant, relief is slow.

05

Texture & wear

OPTIONAL — POST V1
Scratches
A single tiling plate-wear texture, additive, 6% opacity max, applied only to panel/00 faces. Never crosses a text bounding box: reserve a 16px clear margin inside every panel. Scratches follow one diagonal for the whole game — mixed directions read as noise.
Grime
Corner-weighted darkening, 8% max, inside the panel border only. Lives in corners and along the rail seam where a real plate collects dirt. Nothing in the centre third of a panel.
Vegetation shadow
Leaf-silhouette shadow entering from one screen corner, 10% black, drifting 2–3px over 8s. Menu screens only — never the combat HUD, where movement in peripheral vision is a threat signal.
Rift static
Reserved. Only on panels describing an open rift, suppression hardware, or an Anomalous item: 1px teal scanline jitter along the rail, 2-frame flicker every 3–5s. It is a warning, so it must stay rare enough to still be one.
Legibility gate for all four: text contrast must stay above 7:1 against the dirtiest pixel under it, and the whole wear layer must be a single toggle that ships off by default. If a screenshot is unreadable with wear at 100%, the values are wrong, not the screenshot.
