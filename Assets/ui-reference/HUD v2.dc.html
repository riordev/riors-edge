1 · One corner, no chrome
What the player controls sits in a 340-wide stack at 48px from the left and bottom edge; what the player IS sits centred at the bottom of the screen. Height on both is intrinsic rather than fixed — the left stack grows upward from its bottom anchor as effects arrive. The panel plate is gone — no fill, no border, no rail around the group. On a moving gameplay image the readouts themselves are the shapes, so the housing was doing nothing but adding weight. Three rows, 10px apart: the buff and debuff stack on top, momentum, then weapon and abilities at the base nearest the hand. Effects read downward, one per line, newest at the bottom — a vertical column of 8px dot plus 11px value can be counted at a glance, where a horizontal run had to be read.
2 · What was cut
Bottom-right cluster, removed entirely. Shield and health leave the corner entirely: a 232px double bar centred at 56px from the bottom in a 372-wide row (58 + 12 + 232 + 12 + 58), shield 9px over health 6px, with the current value flanking it left and right at 17px in fixed 58px columns and the armour meter centred beneath. Neither carries a max or a label. Levelling sits lower still — a full-width 4px gold rule pinned to the very bottom edge with the level and the raw numbers above it at 12px, so progress is visible without ever competing for the corner. Momentum keeps a 7px track and its state word at 14px, and drops its own panel for a 3px state-coloured rail on the left — 3px is the only rail weight, so a 2px line here would be a fifth border weight with no meaning. Armour is the one place where the vitals block could be mistaken for that track, so it is drawn as a single 88×11 border-box outlined three-cell meter in shield cyan (84×7 interior plus 1px padding and 1px border, box-sizing border-box) rather than loose gold blocks — nothing else in the corner uses gold. Weapon name falls to an 11px mono line above the magazine. Ability squares 56px with 8px gaps — the one ability-square size in the project — and the magazine at 56px, which is number-large, the token named for it. Status effects become a dot and a value on one line.
3 · What holds
Momentum still reports through three channels — colour, fill, and block texture — so the state is peripheral, not read. Ability states are still geometric: 2px accent border ready, hard wedge on cooldown, red struck cost when unaffordable. The 3px momentum rail is the one piece of chrome kept, because it is also the state indicator. Centred vitals sit clear of the crosshair by 400px of dead space, and the XP rule is thin enough at 4px to stay out of the read entirely until it moves. Damage numbers, crosshair, enemy bars, wave banner, and loot popups are unchanged; the banner loses its plate for a single 2px orange underline.
