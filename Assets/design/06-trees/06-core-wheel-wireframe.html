Wheel geometry and rules
Radii from the hub (authored px). Hub 40 (ring 28 inner, 8 dot). Ring 1 Gateway at 160, 48 px square. Ring 2 lanes 240→400, three 24 px round beads at 260 / 330 / 400. Ring 3 Notable at 460, 40 px square; arcs between adjacent lane ends at r 460. Ring 4 Convergence at 550, 48 px diamond. Ring 5 Keystone at 660 on the rim, 64 px diamond. Diamonds are rotated so a corner points along their own radial; their radial half-extent is the half-diagonal (34 / 45 px) and label clearance is computed from it. Rim 2 px border-low at 660. The wheel is N sectors of 360/N°, N from the character's constellation data (five here; a sixth such as Elements is data, not a redesign). Sector n centre at −90° + n·360/N (000 = up); boundaries 1 px panel-1 at centre ± 180/N; lanes at centre ± 24° (clamped to ± (180/N − 12) so a sixth sector still clears its neighbour).
Range card. 72 tick marks every 5° on the rim: 8 px panel-1, every 15° 16 px border-low, sector boundaries 24 px text-2 with a degree label (036 · 108 · 180 · 252 · 324) in numeric 13 at r 690. Sector names sit outside the rim at r 700, 14° clockwise of the centre spoke so they never cross the keystone. Sector names shown are placeholders (dashed underline): the five constellations are the character's own, read from data at runtime. Nothing else is decoration.
Ink outward. Spent nodes and their lanes are sys-full; a spent lane is inked from the hub to its last spent bead, then border-high to the next bead and border-mid beyond. Three-step ladder so unspent never reads as faded: unspent = panel-0 fill, 1 px border-mid, text-2 label; reachable = panel-1 fill, 2 px border-high, text-1 label; spent = sys-full fill. Lanes follow the same ladder: border-mid unspent, border-high reachable, sys-full spent. Rings 1 px panel-1, sector boundaries 1 px panel-2, rim 2 px border-low. Keystone taken = gold-full; its sibling Convergence route is painted, not removed. Notable arcs ink when both ends are spent. Gate marks, drawn. Convergence carries three 12×6 cells on its outward side, one per lane, filled sys-full when that lane's Notable is spent (its gate opens at three). Keystone carries a 16-cell tally, 6×6 cells in two rows of eight, filled sys-full per point spent in the sector toward its gate of 16. Both at 1.0 and 0.6; at 0.6 they are shape-only, no numerals. Cost. At 1.0 a numeric-11 cost sits 8 px past each node's label side, text-2: bead 1, Notable 2, Convergence 3, Keystone 5; Gateway 1. Off at 0.6.
Reading and input. Labels run tangentially, centred on the node's angle, 12 px clear of the node's radial extent: Gateway and first-bead labels on the inward side (toward the hub, where no node sits), Notable and Keystone labels on the outward side; the Convergence label runs along the radial beside its diamond (46 px off the centre spoke, starting at r 550) so it shares no radial with the centre-lane Notable, its cost mirrored on the other side and its gate cells outward toward the Keystone; rotated to read upright on the lower half; only the first bead of a lane is labelled; at 0.6 labels drop and the icon slot carries the node, name in the detail rail. Stick: direction from the current node selects the nearest node within 60° of the stick angle; a tap of the stick steps one ring outward along the current lane. Zoom around the focused node, two levels only; the wheel never rotates.
