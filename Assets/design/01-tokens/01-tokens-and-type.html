Class select · selected class verb hue
Rules. Tints are for rooms only (Forge, Stash, Dialogue, Class select); inventory and every other modal stay olive. A tint replaces the six bg/panel steps on that screen only; borders, text, rails, accents and the hatch stroke keep their olive values. The HUD, nameplates, banners, menus, settings, trees and the control sheets stay olive. Chroma 0.04 is the low end of the permitted 0.04–0.06: at L 0.17–0.35 anything higher clips in sRGB (slate and the class hues already touch the gamut at bg-0).
World behind modals. Every modal that opens over the world (inventory, stash, settings, both trees, death) renders the scene at 40% value (multiply 0.4 post-process, no blur); the modal's plates are opaque. Stand-in scene values at 40%: sky #3E413E, ground #1F210A.
Text on tints. text-3 #8E8D80 keeps 4.5:1 on every tinted bg step and on panel-0 of every tint (lowest: swift panel-0 #1B2A34, 4.9:1); it is not permitted on panel-1 or panel-2 of any tint, as before.

Tokens and type
Field-repaired hardware, not couture. Every value below is a hex, a px, or a rule an engineer can transcribe into Slate without judgement. Depth is border value. Nothing glows.

Warm neutral, slight olive cast (oklch hue 110, chroma 0.006). Never blue. bg-0 is the modal ground; bg-1 the in-world HUD plate; bg-2 sits behind lists.

Text · 4 weights
Full-opacity inks only; never alpha. text-3 passes 4.5:1 on bg-0 (5.4), bg-1 (5.1) and bg-2 (4.6); it is not allowed on any panel step, where labels use text-2 (panel-2: 7.9). text-4 is the painted-disabled ink: a disabled control keeps its border and gets text-4 plus a 45° hatch, it never fades. Focus is always border-high plus a panel step up, never the border alone.

Accent families · 4, plus ultimate
One accent per panel, carried by its rail. Each family has a full ink (text, marks, fills) and a dim ink (tracks, ghosted fills). Dim is a real hex, not opacity. Ability verbs use the same five inks; nothing else may.
