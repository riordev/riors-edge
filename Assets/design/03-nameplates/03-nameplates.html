Scale rule, in pixels. Plates scale from 1.0 at ≤12 m to a 0.5 floor at ≥35 m. Floor: no plate under 5 px tall; fill height inside the borders never under 3 px; boss bar never under 12 px with its 2 px border. Marks stay 12 px at the floor, spacing 4 px.
What drops. Names on Standard, Elite and Champion leave at 20 m. Chip hatch and shield line leave at 25 m: the bar is one fill. Modifier marks stay, at 12 px.
Occlusion and draw distance. Plates hide with the body: if the head is occluded the plate is gone. The boss bar and name draw through occlusion. Maximum draw distance 60 m. Beyond it Standard and Elite draw nothing; Champion draws a 4×4 sys-full contact dot at head height; the boss draws its bar at the 0.5 floor with no name.
Supersedes and deletes. Health-band ticks are deleted in favour of the chip hatch; this supersedes the eight-band rule. The three boss phase marks stay, heavier and independent of health bands. Teal on a name is boss-only; every other name is text-2 (Champion at 20 px). Also deleted: ELITE / CHAMPION labels, band-state text, a screen-top boss bar, per-enemy numbers.
