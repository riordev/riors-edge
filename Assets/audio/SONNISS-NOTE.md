# Sonniss bundles stay out of the repo

Sonniss GDC Game Audio Bundles are multi-gigabyte archives and never enter
the repository. The one found in Downloads on seat 2 was moved, unextracted, to:

    C:\AssetLibrary\Sonniss\Sonniss.com-GDC2024-GameAudioBundle1of9.zip

Curation rule: pick individual files out of the bundle into
Assets/audio/<pack>/ only when a specific sound is needed, with a
LICENSE-NOTE.txt naming the originating library (Sonniss GDC bundles are
royalty-free but per-library attribution files live inside the archive).
(Recorded 2026-08-29.)
